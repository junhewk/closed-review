# Reading a medical support dossier

This is methodological support, not a full review. Begin with the module reconstruction and
coverage/limitations; then inspect observations beside their original source evidence.
The conversation gives up to three priority observations and discloses totals, while the dossier
retains the complete module record.

## Four different results

- Calculated discrepancy: arithmetic differs under the displayed input selections. Verify those
  selections, populations, denominators, units, and applicability before treating it as an error.
- Reporting gap: important information was not located. This is not proof it was not done.
- Methodological concern: an evidence-backed interpretation for the reviewer to assess.
- Not assessable: the manuscript or extraction is insufficient. This is not an adverse finding.

There is no automatic major/minor ranking, model-confirmed validity badge, moral verdict, or
accept/reject recommendation. Exact quotation matching and calculation verification do not
certify scientific meaning. Retain author qualifications, counterevidence and legitimate
methodological alternatives. Do not present every unmet reporting item as a flaw.

## Reviewer control

No sequential acceptance queue is required. Follow-ups, notes and decisions are optional.
Record only words actually supplied by the reviewer, not model-authored reasoning attributed to
them. Accepting an observation does not mean the reviewer wrote its rationale. A general note
need not adopt any observation.

The exported dossier separates model proposals, calculations, withdrawn concerns, limits and
reviewer records. Notes files contain the reviewer's actual words, not automatically drafted
comments to authors/editors. Earlier exports are immutable; generate a new export after changes.

## Manuscript-only boundary

Do not infer unavailable raw data, inspect nonexistent transcripts, verify unread cited sources,
or silently substitute methods. Apply criteria to the stated research tradition and review type.
Language follows user settings; preserve original quotations and statistical notation.
Partial or failed processing never means the manuscript passed a review.
