# Full-length medical source candidates

This is a separate, unreviewed twelve-manuscript development set, not approved medical
qualification evidence. The sources are invented, contain no real participant information and
must not be used as clinical or legal guidance. Each branch has two ordinary-length candidates.

The manifest stays `independently_reviewed: false`. Source hashes, parsing, deliberate author
edits and the presence of a contrasting candidate do not establish clean controls, valid seeded
issues, representativeness or reviewer judgments. A qualified human must decide those matters
independently. No intended-issue map, control assignment or expected-issue key is encoded in
the manifest or filenames.

Use the [source-only review procedure](../support-ordinary-draft/SOURCE-REVIEW.md) for
coherence, sufficient printed information, branch-appropriate interpretation and intended-domain
representativeness. Keep reviewer identity/date/reasons and all proposed issue decisions on the
evaluator machine, not in the key-free manifest. Any author's issue proposals are unapproved
drafts, not independent answer keys; they are held separately outside this repository.

The original six drafts and their frozen engineering batch remain unchanged. This new set has
not been transferred to the model host or run through inference. Do not replace the running
batch or relabel its unreviewed-at-start manifest. After actual independent source/test-role
approval, freeze a **new** approved corpus manifest before three paired support/baseline
repetitions. Keep private issue maps, judgments and checking times off the model host. Do not
reuse legacy scan keys or infer approval from a successful parser/calculator.

These are candidate materials for later human selection, not a claim that twelve related
discharge-care examples sufficiently represent every medical method or specialty.

유효한 삽입 오류, 의학적 타당성 또는 실제 진료·법률 지침으로 승인되지 않았습니다.
먼저 위 원고 자체를 읽고 일관성, 원고만으로 판단 가능한 범위와 시험 목적에의 적합성을
독립적으로 검토해 주세요. 이후 별도로 보관한 작성자의 제안도 검토자가 확인·수정·기각해야
합니다. 작성자의 의도는 정답이 아닙니다. 검토자 이름·날짜·근거와 정답·대조군 표시는 평가자
전용 위치에만 보관하며 모델 서버로 보내지 않습니다.

현재 실행 중인 여섯 원고의 개발 시험은 그대로 유지됩니다. 이 열두 후보 원고는 아직
모델 서버에 전송하거나 추론 시험에 사용하지 않았습니다. 독립 검토 후 새 승인 원고 집합과
해시를 확정한 다음 새 평가를 시작해야 하며, 기존 시험을 소급 승인할 수 없습니다.
