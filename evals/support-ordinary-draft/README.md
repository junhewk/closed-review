# Ordinary-length synthetic corpus development

This is an incomplete development corpus, **not qualification evidence**. The manuscripts are
invented and contain no real participant information. They provide connected clinical methods,
tables, results and discussion rather than repeated traversal filler.

The manifest remains `independently_reviewed: false`. An independently qualified human must
review each source for coherence, mathematical/methodological intent and representativeness
before any formal qualification batch. A draft must not be called a clean control simply
because its author intended it to be coherent. Inadvertent errors remain possible.
The [source-only review worksheet](SOURCE-REVIEW.md) links all six originals and separates
source approval from private issue adjudication. Its decisions are deliberately unfilled.

Only key-free manuscripts and the manifest may go to the model host. Control assignments,
intended-issue proposals and later expert judgments belong in a separate evaluator-held packet.
None are encoded in the manifest or manuscript filenames. Do not reuse legacy scan keys.

Six manuscripts now cover every branch: statistics (3,777 words), qualitative study (4,091 words),
systematic review (4,278 words), scoping review (3,875 words), narrative review (3,746 words)
and logic (3,850 words). The qualitative study includes participant construction,
an explicit coding approach, contrasting accounts and excerpt-to-theme links. The systematic
review distinguishes search records, included reports and trials, with explicit report linkage,
different outcome definitions and study-level appraisal rationales. These are authored examples,
not independently validated findings or proof that their methodological choices are appropriate.
The scoping review includes source-level mapping and overlapping conceptual categories, without
claiming clinical effectiveness or treating source counts as patient prevalence.
The narrative review separates conceptual, descriptive, associative and intervention claims.
The logic paper separates a fictional charter, stipulated capacity, normative premises and
objection–reply links without asserting actual law or a universal moral verdict.
A complete independently reviewed medical corpus
must still cover both comparison and seeded material for **every** branch: statistics,
qualitative study, systematic review, scoping review, narrative review and logic. Three paired
repetitions, baselines, human coverage/validity review and checking times remain required.
These drafts cover the branch IDs but do not satisfy those requirements. No clean/seeded
assignment, private expected-issue key, independent source approval or human quality judgment
has been created from the fact that a draft parses successfully.

A separate [twelve-source candidate set](../support-ordinary-candidates/README.md) now supplies
two unreviewed drafts per branch for later independent selection. Its author proposals remain
outside this repository on the evaluator machine, not in the manifest or model-host inputs.
Neither set has approved control/issue roles. The original six sources and running engineering
batch are unchanged; do not replace them or retroactively approve their outputs.
